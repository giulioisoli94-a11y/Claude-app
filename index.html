    Use it on your own stocks

    1. Download daily prices as CSV: on Yahoo Finance open a ticker → Historical Data → Download, or use stooq.com, or export from your broker. You need Date, Open, High, Low, Close and ideally Volume columns.
    2. Use at least 1 year of daily data so the 200-day average and older support levels exist. Switch to Weekly or Monthly to read the bigger picture (monthly needs about 5+ years of daily data).
    3. Click Load CSV…. Everything runs in your browser; the file is not uploaded anywhere.
    4. Click a setup on the right to draw it, see a trade plan and check it against the confirmation list. Click a triangle marker on the chart to open that candle pattern.
    5. Click Backtest this chart to replay the scanner bar by bar over the whole history of the stock and see how each setup actually played out there.

    Patterns describe tendencies, not certainties. The scanner follows textbook rules measured in ATR (average true range); it will miss some patterns and see some that a human would reject. Use it to train your eye and to structure a plan, then make your own decision.
